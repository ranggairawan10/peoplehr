ACT TWO · DIAGNOSTIC
08
The Thinking Organisation
08 / 14
ranggairawan.com

Four questions. Any fuzzy answer means the decision will get stuck.

01
AUTHORITY
Who can say no · and have it stick?
If three people can each veto and none can approve alone, you do not have an owner. You have a queue.
02
ACCESS
Who sees the data first, in full, on time?
If the owner gets a sanitised summary two days after the room, the owner is decorative.
03
MEASUREMENT
Whose number moves when this decision goes well or badly?
If no one's KPI changes either way, the decision has no consequence · and no owner.
04
CONTINUITY
Who is still accountable in six months?
If the owner rotates every quarter or sits outside the executing function, accountability evaporates on contact.

The eighty percent of cost you do not measure is the cost that decides execution.

ABOVE THE WATERLINE
≈20%
What finance sees.
External recruitment cost. Training spend to close late-identified gaps. Consultants hired for work the internal team should be able to do.
BELOW THE WATERLINE
≈80%
What the business actually pays for.
Execution cost. Opportunity cost. Retention cost. Decision quality cost. Strategic delay cost. None of it hits a P&L line.
RULE OF THUMB
If you only manage what finance measures, you are managing twenty percent of your true cost.
